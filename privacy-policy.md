# Privacy Policy for Roamie

Effective Date: June 11, 2026

Roamie (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

## Information We Collect

### Location Information

Roamie uses your device location to provide location-based recommendations, nearby area comparisons, and personalized discovery experiences.

Location data is only used to provide app functionality and is not sold to third parties.

### Sign In Information

Roamie supports Sign in with Apple for user authentication.
When you sign in, Apple may share limited account information such as your Apple ID email address or anonymous relay email.

### Device and Usage Information

The App may collect limited technical information required for app functionality, performance improvement, and crash diagnostics.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

* Provide location-based features
* Improve app performance and user experience
* Authenticate users securely
* Maintain and operate app services

## Data Sharing

Roamie does not sell personal information or user data to third parties.

Information may only be shared:

* when required by law,
* to protect the security and integrity of the service,
* or with trusted service providers necessary to operate the app.

## Data Retention

User data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Children’s Privacy

Roamie is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
